📚 Complete Abbreviations & Terminology
Market & Trading
NYMEX: New York Mercantile Exchange - where oil futures trade
WTI: West Texas Intermediate - type of crude oil (standard US grade)
RBOB: Reformulated Gasoline Blendstock - gasoline futures
ULSD / HO: Ultra Low Sulfur Diesel / Heating Oil - diesel futures
Spread: Price difference between two products/locations (e.g., Midland vs Cushing)
MtM: Mark-to-Market - current profit/loss on open position
Data Sources & Systems
EIA: US Energy Information Administration - official energy data
MPR / MIPS: Marathon Petroleum forecasts - internal production & sales
Kpler: Vessel tracking - real tanker flows and movements
TMR: Trading Management System - deal tracking and execution records
RDW: Risk Data Warehouse - P&L tracking and positions
TRR: Transportation Rate database - shipping cost data
Technical & ML Terms
LightGBM: Fast ML algorithm - learns patterns from historical data
Feature: Meaningful data point the model uses ("inventory +5%", "price moved 2 sigma")
Sharpe Ratio: Reward per unit of risk (1.94x = earning $1.94 for every $1 of risk)
Z-Score: Measure of how unusual something is (3.0 = very unusual)
Precision: Percentage of BUY signals that make money (63% of ours do)
Backtesting: Testing strategy on historical data to see if it would've made money
Strategy Types
Time Arbitrage: Buy near-term, sell far-term to profit from price curve differences
Geo Arbitrage: Buy in one location, sell in another for regional price differences
Crack Spread: Buy 3 crude, sell 2 gasoline + 1 diesel (refinery margin trade)
Contango: Market where future prices higher than spot (storage profitable)
Backwardation: Market where future prices lower than spot (scarcity signal)
Infrastructure & Cloud
Azure: Microsoft cloud - where our system runs
Databricks: Big data platform - runs ML models and feature engineering
Delta Lake: Data storage format - organized data with full history
PostgreSQL: Database for fast lookups (signals, P&L)
FastAPI: Backend server that delivers signals to dashboard
React.js: JavaScript framework - the interactive dashboard traders use
